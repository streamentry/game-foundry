# Quiet Shot — independent design (playtest v0.1)

**Positioning:** a precise, calm ricochet puzzle. Choose one direction, commit, watch the result, learn from a miss. This is about playful judgment, not medical cognition claims.

**Three adjectives:** airy · deliberate · satisfying.

## Semantic tokens

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Paper | `#F2F7F7` |
| Board | `#E7F1F0` |
| Ink | `#183C45` |
| Muted text | `#4C6570` |
| Primary action | `#247A8B` |
| Mirror | `#355C70` |
| Unhit targets | `#E7AA63` |
| Cleared targets | `#5A9E8A` |
| Ray trail | `#196E83` |

**Type:** system typeface, 29px headline, 18px outcome, 16px buttons and aim, 12–15px supporting. **Contrast:** dark text on pale backgrounds, white on deep-blue action. Cleared targets include a check mark and a numerical result; status does not depend on color alone. Contrast and screen reader verification are not yet complete on native devices.

**Spacing:** 18px horizontal inset, 12px rhythm, 9px between buttons. Board at least 352 logical px high; buttons at least 54px.

**Motion:** immediate trajectory reveal; no forced wait or timers, no score pressure. A later optional 150–350ms stroke animation could be tested only if it improves comprehension.

**Sound/haptics:** none initially. No unexpected audio or forced vibration. **Puzzle feedback:** ray path drawn after fire, hit marks on individual targets, text that reports cleared/total. Failure is explained through a transparent outcome, not an arbitrary "wrong" banner.

**Icon brief:** a single teal arrow-like light ray bending once off a thin dark-blue bar, with one amber circular target. Avoid aesthetic resemblance to the visual identity of existing ricochet titles.

**Screenshots proposed:** initial board and guide, a miss with visible trace, a three-target win, and angle controls on a compact phone. These are future original screenshots, not current device-verified assets.

**Accessibility:** allow tapping/dragging anywhere on the board to aim; independent ±5° and ±1° buttons avoid precise angular swipes. Keyboard left/right and Enter as an alternative. No color-only feedback. Board is a custom-drawn visual and is **not yet a full nonvisual screen-reader play interface**; this limitation requires user testing and potentially additional accessible controls.

**Originality:** independently authored eight boards and rendering; never copy level layouts, assets, names or trade dress from Okay? or other established physics puzzles.
