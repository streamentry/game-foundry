## Gaps this search could not close

- No query of any official trademark register returned a result. USPTO, WIPO, EUIPO, JPO and the UK IPO full register are all unverified.
- App Store and Google Play were searched only through general web search, not the store search functions.
- No search of Steam, itch.io, Nintendo/Sony/Microsoft stores, or game-industry databases beyond a few web hits.
- No company registry checks (US state, UK Companies House, Japan).
- No domain or social-handle check beyond the existence of the project's own mosselora.com.
- No review of common-law use in Japan (Japanese katakana/kana phonetic equivalents were not searched).

## Recommendation for professional clearance

Commission a trademark attorney to run a full clearance covering:

1. **Jurisdictions, in order:** US first (USPTO federal register, state registers, common law); then UK (UKIPO register, Trade Mark Journal); then Japan (JPO, including katakana/kana forms and Japanese-language common use), per the AGENTS.md market order (US English first, UK follow-up, Japan first localization candidate). Add EU (EUIPO) if the EU storefront is in scope.
2. **Nice classes:** 9 (downloadable game software, mobile apps), 28 (games and toys, board and puzzle games), 41 (entertainment services, online games, providing non-downloadable games). Consider 42 (software as a service) only if the web research slice or world API is branded.
3. **Variants:** Mosselora, Mossalora, Moss Lora, Mosslora, Mosselore, Moselora, plus phonetic equivalents and the Evergrove codename where relevant to store listings.
4. **Marks to examine closely:** MOSSORA (Shenzhen Mossora Technology Co., Ltd.) across classes 9, 28 and 41, including any pending or newly filed applications; MOSSETTA; any "Mossel-" / "Mosse-" / "Mosel-" word marks in classes 9, 28, 41.
5. **App stores:** exhaustive Apple App Store and Google Play search (titles, subtitles, developer names), plus Steam, itch.io and console stores.
6. **Companies and domains:** registries in the US, UK and Japan; domain and social-handle availability for mosselora.com and related names.
7. **Opinion scope:** likelihood of confusion, descriptiveness, and whether a coexistence or alternative-name path is needed. Obtain a written opinion before any public trademark claim or store submission.

Until this clearance is complete, keep "Mosselora" as an internal/working name and do not announce trademark clearance (consistent with `DECISIONS_AND_RISKS.md`).
