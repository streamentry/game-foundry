# Family co-play session (draft protocol)

**Purpose:** the cheapest test of the founder's family goal ([vision §1a](../../vision.md#1a-family-play-connection-not-only-entertainment)). Do families already play the current build **together** on one device, and do they want to do it again? No new feature is needed to run it.

**Status:** blank instrument. No sessions, participants or results are recorded here. Values marked `TO PREDECLARE` must be fixed in a dated [decision-log](../../../../docs/research/decision-log.md) entry before the first group.

This is **not** the M3 gate. M3 stays an uncoached solo study of adults 40+ ([m3-session-kit.md](m3-session-kit.md)). Run family sessions alongside it, not in place of it.

## Who

- **5 family groups** (`TO PREDECLARE`: final count). Each group: one adult 40+ (record band: 40–49, 50–64 or 65+) and one child aged 6–12 (record band: 6–8 or 9–12). A parent may join.
- Groups who already spend time together. Do not pair strangers.
- Record nothing that identifies anyone in this repository. Use codes such as `F-01`, and keep consent forms off-repo ([playtest privacy](README.md#privacy)).

## Consent (children)

- A parent or legal guardian gives written consent for the child. The child is also asked in plain words and may stop at any time without a reason.
- No audio or video of children unless the guardian opts in separately. Default to written observation notes.
- `TO PREDECLARE`: who reviews this consent wording before the first group.

## Setup

- One phone or tablet with the current release-candidate build. Tablet and phone are both acceptable; record which.
- Hand the device to the **adult**, say only: *"This is a game you can play together. Play however you like for about 15 minutes."* Give no other instruction.
- The facilitator does not suggest turn-taking, does not explain the rules, and answers questions with *"What do you think?"*

## Observe (tally on the sheet, with timestamps where useful)

| Code | What to note |
| --- | --- |
| **Touch** | Who touches the screen, and roughly what share of the boards each person plays |
| **Hand-off** | Does the device change hands without prompting? Who starts it? |
| **Choice talk** | At the tree / bridge / flowers choice, does one person ask the other? Quote the words if heard |
| **Help** | Who helps whom, and how (pointing, explaining, taking over) |
| **Disengage** | Time when either person looks away for more than 30 seconds, leaves, or asks to stop |
| **Friction** | Anything that blocks shared play: tile size, reading load, a child tapping too fast, a grandparent rushed |

## Ask afterwards (each person separately, short)

1. *"What did you like doing together?"* (open)
2. *"Would you play this together again?"* Yes / maybe / no, then *"Why?"*
3. Adult only: *"Would you show this to anyone else in your family?"* Who (relationship only, no names)?
4. Child only: *"Was it too easy, too hard, or just right?"*

Do not ask about loneliness or wellbeing. This session records behaviour and stated intent only.

## Decision rules (`TO PREDECLARE` before the first group)

Proposed defaults:

- **Continue to a take-turns prototype** if at least 3 of 5 groups have both people take part without a prompt, **and** at least 3 of 5 adults say "yes" to playing together again.
- **Redesign before building co-play** if children disengage within 5 minutes in 3 or more groups, or if adults take over the device in most groups.
- **Drop co-play as a near-term feature** (keep the family goal as a value, revisit after M4) if fewer than 2 of 5 groups play together at all.
