# Run 3 (I338 onward): player notes and the PM's call

The founder restarted the 100-iteration goal on 2026-11-27, after the 50 world-games ideas (I290–I337). The cycle is: play as a player and take notes, decide as PM, then build and release five iterations per PR.

**What these notes are:** one team member playing the b309 build (all 50 ideas) from first launch through the daily loop, as a first-time player in their fifties would, from the simulator tour's 109 screens. These are team observations, not evidence from adults 40+. That still needs the [daily-loop kit](daily-loop-kit.md).

## PM: what comes first

Ranked by how often a player meets it, and how much it gets in the way of the vision (calm, icon-led, family-centred):

1. **I338** Family send becomes three calm steps: gift, then wrap, then words (P1).
2. **I339** Wrap choices as pictures only, no "No envelope" or "No stamp" text (P2).
3. **I340** The basket token looks like a basket, with the newest thing peeking out (P3).
4. **I341** The fan's Look token, and the garden-file actions as painted icon tokens (P4, P5).
5. **I342** Talking to a neighbour leads with the neighbour: portrait and words first, the story below (P6).

Later batches pick up P7–P10, then the deeper work of this run: the daily return, and a garden that feels like yours sooner.
