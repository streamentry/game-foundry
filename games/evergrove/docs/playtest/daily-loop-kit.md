# Daily-loop kit: three days with the I189–I288 build

**What this is:** a blank instrument for the question the I189–I288 run could not answer itself: do adults 40+ understand the daily loop, choose to come back, and (for families) play together? It covers what the [M3 kit](m3-session-kit.md) does not: puzzle kinds and hints, the river's drops, notes, family features and co-play.

**Nothing here is evidence until sessions are run.** Thresholds marked `TO PREDECLARE` must be fixed in writing, in [decision-log.md](../../../../docs/research/decision-log.md), before the first participant is recruited. Privacy rules from the [packet README](README.md) apply: participant codes only, nothing identifying in the repository.

## Who

- 9 to 12 adults who already play casual puzzles on their own phone, split across bands **40–49, 50–64 and 65+** (3 to 4 each). Report each band separately; never pool to "40+".
- Optional: 2 to 3 grandparent and grandchild pairs (child 7+), for the co-play part only, under the [family co-play protocol](family-coplay-session.md) (consent, no child data).

## Build

- A current development build from `main` on the participant's own iPhone (TestFlight or a cable install), with the research diary on, as in the [measurement decision](../specs/proposals/measurement-decision-2026-11-24.md). Record the commit hash.
- Garden notes are **not** pre-enabled; whether a player turns them on is part of the test.

## Day 1: a 30-minute session, observed, uncoached

Start from the title screen; say only "This is a garden game; play as you would at home." Record each item as yes, no or not reached, with time where marked.

| # | Observe | Feature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1-1 | Opens a thing's puzzle without help (time to first open) | play marks (I221, I255) |
| D1-2 | Solves a new-kind puzzle (any of the five) without help; uses the hint? | picture rules and hints (I253, I254) |
| D1-3 | Says, unprompted or when asked "what are those marks?", that marks show different puzzles | kind badges (I255) |
| D1-4 | Explains the drops on the mist when asked "what do those drops mean?" | river drops (I224, I225) |
| D1-5 | Buys a nursery plant; notices the book mark | nursery and almanac (I238–I240) |
| D1-6 | Reads a neighbour's line; asked "is that about your garden?", says yes and why | truthful companion (I210, I257) |
| D1-7 | If the soft ask appears: turns notes on, declines, or ignores | notes (I276) |
| D1-8 | Any moment of confusion, frustration or delight (verbatim, timed) | all |

## Days 2 and 3: at home, unobserved

- No reminders from the team. The phone's notes are whatever the player chose.
- The research diary records only local open dates (no content, never leaves the phone).
- End of day 3, a 10-minute call:
  - Did you come back? What brought you back?
  - If notes were on: did they feel welcome, or like pressure? Read the last one aloud if remembered.
  - What would you change?

## Co-play pairs (optional, 20 minutes, observed)

- Hand the phone to the pair. Mention only: "There's a two-people button on the puzzle card."
- Observe:
  - they take turns without being told how;
  - the child stays engaged for 5 minutes;
  - the adult takes over;
  - either mentions the two-colour mark afterwards.

## Results, by band, with denominators

Use [results-template.md](results-template.md). Thresholds are `TO PREDECLARE`. Suggested starting points only, to argue about before recruiting:

| Question | Suggested threshold | Stop or rework if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opens a puzzle unaided (D1-1) | `TO PREDECLARE` (suggest 8 of 10 within 60 s) | fewer than 6 of 10 |
| Solves a new kind with at most one hint (D1-2) | `TO PREDECLARE` | a kind failed by most players: move it to a later edition or cut it |
| Explains the drops (D1-4) | `TO PREDECLARE` | most can't: redesign the drops or Mira's line |
| Returns on day 2 or 3 without a team reminder | `TO PREDECLARE` (decide before seeing data) | below the predeclared bar after two honest iterations |
| Notes kept on through day 3 | `TO PREDECLARE` | more than 1 in 4 turn them off, or anyone calls a note pressure (the [spec's](../specs/proposals/notifications-2026-11-27.md) own stop rule) |
| Co-play pairs take turns unprompted | `TO PREDECLARE` (the family protocol suggests 2 of 5) | children disengage within 5 minutes in most pairs |

## Never claim from this kit

Retention at scale; wellbeing, memory or loneliness effects; that families play together in general. Report what these participants did, by band.
