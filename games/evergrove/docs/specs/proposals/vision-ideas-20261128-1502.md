## 1. An axle chapter, gentle to firm (puzzle depth)

**Vision:** §2.2 "depth from a system… editorially reviewed difficulty curves". Seven runs measured mechanics; none has asked whether a mechanic can fill an ordered chapter.

**Player value:** a future chapter of linked-gate boards that never needs a guess and gets steadily firmer, so the axle is learned on easy boards and used on harder ones.

**Counterargument:** an order by a solver statistic is not a felt difficulty curve; only players can say what "firm" is.

**Cheapest disproof:** if seeds 0–59 cannot supply 12 guess-free, axle-restarting boards with distinct difficulty steps, linked gates can't carry a chapter alone.

**Files:** new `Assets/Scripts/Core/LinkedForkChapter.cs`; new `Tests/LinkedForkChapterTests.cs`; register in `Tests/Program.cs`.

**Acceptance (pure C#):** `LinkedForkChapter.Draw(int length, int seedLimit)` returns seeds, deterministic; every board is guess-free (`AxleMoment(...).SolvedWithoutGuess`), the axle restarts deduction, truth kept; ordered by work after the stall (`Cells − FixedBeforeStall`) ascending, ties by seed; no repeated seed. *(Review change: taking the first 12 of that ranking gave work 4 twelve times, so `Draw` spaces its picks evenly through the ranking instead; see the [run log](../../vision-runs/20261128-1502.md).)* The test locks the chapter for (12, 60), and prints the work-after-stall sequence. Nothing visible: not wired into play.

**Art/feel:** none yet. If it passes, the first board of the chapter is the one to paint with a visible shaft.

## 2. A chosen thing says you finished its puzzle (truthful village memory)

**Vision:** §3 "the place remembers it truthfully". Nursery plants already say "You grew it from its puzzle."; a chosen thing solved alone says nothing about its puzzle, and the together sentence appears without it.

**Player value:** holding a thing on the map tells the whole true story: when it was chosen, how many streams have passed, whether you finished its puzzle, and whether you did it together.

**Counterargument:** one more sentence in a caption; players may not hold things at all.

**Cheapest disproof:** if `WorldModel.TreeSolved(choiceIndex)` is not the record of the chosen thing's puzzle, the sentence can't be truthful and the idea dies.

**Files:** `Assets/Scripts/Core/ThingStory.cs`; `Tests/ThingStoryTests.cs`.

**Acceptance (pure C#):** chosen-thing line adds " You finished its puzzle." only when the save records it solved; "You solved it together." only after it, and only when also solved (as the nursery line). Unsolved: unchanged line. Puzzle-only/matched modes still "". Visible on screen: the long-press caption on a chosen thing (Living mode, English).

**Art/feel:** words only, kept short; "finished", not "grew", because a chosen thing can be a bridge.

## 3. Solved together, everyone's light comes on (family play, one device)

**Vision:** §1a "cooperative only… everyone helps the same garden". Today a board solved together pulses only the token; the seat pips stay as they were and a screen reader hears nothing about the shared finish.

**Player value:** at the end of a shared board every seat's pip lights at once, in order round the table, and the screen reader says "Solved together, all three of you." The finish belongs to everyone, with no counts per person.

**Counterargument:** a small flourish on an already busy finish; families may not notice.

**Cheapest disproof:** a family session where nobody looks at the token at the finish.

**Files:** `Assets/Scripts/Core/TakeTurns.cs` (`SpokenSolved`); `Assets/Scripts/Unity/TreePlay.Turns.cs` (`TableSolved`); one line in `Assets/Scripts/Unity/TreePlay.cs` replaced one for one; tests in a new `Tests/SolvedTogetherTests.cs`.

**Acceptance:** `SpokenSolved(1)` is "" (alone, nothing new); 2 → "Solved together, both of you."; 3 → "Solved together, all three of you."; 4 → "…all four of you."; out of range clamps, never throws. A source check pins the wiring. On screen: all active pips filled at the finish, each pulsing once with a small stagger; with motion off they fill at once, no pulse.

**Art/feel:** pips fill in EarthInk as the current turn's pip does; stagger 0.06 s; reduced-motion safe.

## 4. Evergreens keep their green (art and feel)

**Vision:** taste charter §1.2 "hand-made" and §3.3 "seasons repaint the map". Every shade tree blossoms in spring, turns in autumn and rests bare in winter, including the holly, yew, pines, cedar, cypress, juniper, bay, olive and citrus. A bare holly in winter reads as careless to an adult gardener.

**Player value:** a winter garden where the evergreens you chose stay green among bare branches: truer, and more varied to look at.

**Counterargument:** few players will notice which tree is which; extra cases in a seasonal rule.

**Cheapest disproof:** a stand-in player shown two winter screenshots can't tell which is "right".

**Files:** `Assets/Scripts/Core/SeasonLook.cs`; `Assets/Scripts/Core/GardenCatalog.cs` (an evergreen list, by tree index); `Assets/Scripts/Unity/GardenMapView.cs` (pass the tree's catalogue index); `Tests/SeasonLookTests.cs`.

**Acceptance (pure C#):** evergreen trees are `Full` in every season (no blossom, no turning, no resting); deciduous trees unchanged; flowers unchanged; unknown index (−1) behaves as today. The list is exactly: olive, lemon, orange, juniper, Scots pine, cedar, cypress, yew, holly, bay laurel. On screen: in winter those trees draw full, without the resting tint.

**Art/feel:** no new art; the full painting is the evergreen look. Palette and motion unchanged.

## 5. A thing's water board says how far the water reaches (accessibility)

**Vision:** §2.7 "readable and touchable for the people we design for"; the story board shows "Water reaches n/m tiles", but a thing's water board has only colour (the wet frontier) to say it.

**Player value:** a screen-reader player hears "Water reaches 5 of 9 pipes." as they turn tiles on a thing's board, and "Water runs through every pipe." when it is solved.

**Counterargument:** screen-reader use among the audience is unmeasured; the line could be noise.

**Cheapest disproof:** a VoiceOver user finds the line repeats too often to bear.

**Files:** new `Assets/Scripts/Core/WaterReachWords.cs`; new `Assets/Scripts/Unity/TreePlay.Reach.cs`; one line in `TreePlay.cs` `Paint()` replaced one for one; tests in a new `Tests/WaterReachWordsTests.cs`.

**Acceptance:** `WaterReachWords.Line(wet, total)`: total ≤ 0 → ""; wet clamped to 0..total; wet == total → "Water runs through every pipe."; else "Water reaches {wet} of {total} pipes." (and "1 pipe" grammar for total 1 is not needed; total 1 is always complete). The hidden label is one node, reused, updated on each paint, English only. Nothing visible changes.

**Art/feel:** invisible by design.
