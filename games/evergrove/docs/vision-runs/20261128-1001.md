**Idea 1 result (the cheapest disproof was triggered):** `RootedDepth` runs arc-consistency deduction and a deduce-then-guess search on `RootedBoards`. For seeds 0–199: one root, 200 boards, all 15 pipes forced, 0 guesses on every board; two roots, 200 boards, all 14 pipes forced, 0 guesses on every board. Root stones change a board's shape but add no reasoning. Depth proposal C1 alone won't solve vision §2.2; the next candidates are constraints that remove local forcing (ecology rules that make a tile's ports depend on something other than its neighbours, or larger boards with more open regions). Measure any such mechanic with `RootedDepth`/`DeductionDepth` before drawing art.

**Idea 2 result:** the save-derived lines (protected-pipe cause, lantern letter, memory, consequence, journal) were already truthful after a reconsider. The implementer noticed that `ReconsiderLatestChoice` did not trim the `together` and `helped`/`helpedFrom` marks. Reconsider is allowed right after the thing's own puzzle is solved, so a "solved together" or "helped by Nan" mark stayed on that index. The next thing chosen inherited it, and a save written in between failed `ReadHelped` validation on reload (helped indexes must be below the number of choices), which sends the player into backup recovery. Fixed with `WorldModel.ForgetGrownMarks`. The new test fails without the fix ("'solved together' leaves with the thing") and passes with it. No save-schema change.

**Idea 3 rework:** the first version stepped the turn back once per undo. The implementer's audit found boards that record undo steps without passing the turn: lifting a piece and the rotate/mirror steps (`PiecesBoard`), reshuffles (`MahjongBoard`), multi-step hints (`DoorstepBoard`, `PiecesBoard`) and the water board's head start. Undo would then have taken the turn from the wrong person. The coordinator replaced it with `TurnLedger`: each passed turn is noted at its undo step, and undo/redo look the turn up by step. Pre-existing and unchanged: the mosaic board passes the turn on selection as well as on the swap.

**Idea 4 narrowed:** a `SeasonContrastTests` suite already holds body text at 4.5:1 on Paper, CardFill and the action tones in every season, by parsing `MosseloraTheme.cs`. The proposed single-source refactor broke that parser and duplicated it, so it was reverted. `ThemeContrastTests` covers only the pairs not yet tested: both pipe channels on all six tile states (3:1; lowest DryChannel on HintFocusTile 3.39:1), ink and secondary ink on six panels (4.5:1; lowest 5.24:1), and the landmark frame. No colour changed.
