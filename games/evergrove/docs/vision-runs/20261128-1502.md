**Idea 1 result.** As proposed, the draw took the 12 lowest-work boards, and all 12 had work 4: a flat chapter. That was a fault in the proposal, not the code. The coordinator changed `Draw` to space its picks evenly through the ranking, so the first board is the gentlest and the last the firmest. Of seeds 0–59, 47 qualify (guess-free, the axle restarts deduction, truth kept). Their work after the stall is spread 4×20, 6×12, 8×10, 9×2, 12, 13, 17. The chapter is seeds 5, 15, 24, 35, 46, 2, 11, 37, 20, 47, 4, 3 with work 4,4,4,4,4,6,6,6,8,8,9,17. That is a real curve, but its firm end is thin: three boards above 9, and a jump from 9 to 17 at the close. A full linked chapter probably needs more seeds or a second mechanic for its last boards. The test locks all of this, and also requires that the chapter opens on the minimum and closes on the maximum work with at least three steps.

**Idea 2 review.** `ThingStory.For` now passes `world.TreeSolved(choiceIndex)`. Reconsidering a choice trims `treeSolved` (`WorldModel.ReconsiderLatestChoice`), so a replaced thing can't inherit the old thing's solve. "Together" is said only with "finished", as nursery lines do. The word is "finished", not "grew", because a chosen thing can be a bridge.

**Idea 3 review.** One line in `TreePlay.Finish` became `TableSolved();`, keeping the old token pulse. Pips fill in `EarthInk`, the same as the current-turn pip. They pulse only when motion is on. The turn caption node is reused. Nothing repaints the pips after a solve (`FlipTurn` and `ToggleTogether` return when solved). No per-person counts.

**Idea 4 review.** The evergreen list is matched by name against the tree catalogue, not by index. Map items now carry a tree's catalogue index in `FlowerIndex` (comment updated), including basket plantings of trees. The only reader that cares about flowers, the resting-flower shrink, was already guarded by family.

**Idea 5 review.** Every cell of a water board is a pipe (`PipeKind` is End, Straight or Corner), so the total is the cell count and the wet count is `PipeInsight.ConnectedCount`. One line in `TreePlay.Paint` became the same line plus `SpeakReach(insight);`. The caption is English only and is one reused node. `TreePlay.cs` stays at 1,099 lines.
