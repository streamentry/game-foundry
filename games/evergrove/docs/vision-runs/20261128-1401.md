**Idea 1 result.** For seeds 0–59, neighbour-only deduction (no axle) fixes 4 to 21 of 25 cells before it stalls, median 19. On 55 of 60 boards, one axle prune then lets neighbour deduction fix more cells. 47 of 60 then finish with no guess, and those are exactly the 47 guess-free boards from 1301. So on most boards the axle matters late, after the player has already worked out most of the garden. That is the shape of an "aha", not bookkeeping. On 5 boards the axle does nothing at the stall point (they need a guess anyway). The test locks all these numbers. The refactor kept the 1301 numbers exactly: 60 boards need the link, 13 need a guess, and no board needs more than 4 guesses.

**Idea 2, why it was dropped.** After the story, the season turns every few *solves*, and solves include nursery-plant puzzles, so "Autumn came with stream 19" could name a puzzle that wasn't a stream. The save also doesn't record the solve count when the story ended. A season turn caused by finishing the story could then be credited to the wrong puzzle. Making it truthful needs a new save field (a v4 additive field plus a migration test), which is more than one small idea. Reverted entirely; nothing ships.

**Idea 3 review.** Two older source checks (`TurnBackTests`, `TableSetTests`) pinned the old line `turn = 0; ledger.Clear(); RememberTable();`. They now pin `ResizeTable(); RememberTable();` and the ledger note at the current undo step. Going to "alone" still clears the ledger. Undo past a resize uses the old turn modulo the new table size, so it always stays in range.

**Idea 4 review.** `gentle` (from `gentleMotion`) means motion is on; the same flag gates the pip pulse and bloom. With motion off, or an inactive token, the colour is set at once. Only one fade runs at a time. `TreePlay.cs` stays at 1,099 lines (guarded).

**Idea 5 fix.** The implementer found that a tap only repaints the board; it doesn't rebuild it. A caption fixed at build time would have described the tile's old orientation after the first turn, so the implementer rightly left it unwired. The coordinator added `LongPressCaption.AttachLive`, which reads the words when the caption shows and falls back to the fixed text. The tile caption uses it in English only. A source check in `TileOpeningsTests` pins the wiring.
