**Idea 1 result (the proposal's cheapest disproof was triggered):** arc consistency forces every rotatable tile on every authored board. The base boards give 9 of 9 (3×3) and 16 of 16 (4×4). The variants give 8 of 8 and 15 of 15, because the protected tile is not counted. Edge pruning alone forces 4–6 of 9 tiles on 3×3 and 8 of 16 on 4×4; neighbour agreement closes the rest. So **no authored board needs lookahead or guessing.** This is honest evidence that today's boards have shallow logical depth, which matches the vision's §2.2 "unsolved". The number can't rank today's boards. It can show whether a future mechanic (root stones, ecology) makes a board need real reasoning. Next step for depth: measure `RootedBoards` boards with `DeductionDepth`, then add a search-depth measure for boards that local deduction can't finish.
