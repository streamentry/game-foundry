**Idea 1 result.** `WorkByReach(240)` over the 197 qualifying axle boards, as `{reach, boards, total work}`: {2,48,302} {3,62,355} {4,51,338} {5,25,163} {6,9,58} {7,1,8} {8,1,8}. Mean work is 6.29, 5.73, 6.63, 6.52 and 6.44 at reach 2 to 6. Reach 7 and 8 have one board each, too few to read. How far apart the tied tiles sit doesn't set the difficulty, so it can't serve as a visible dial. Work level (run 1701) stays the only lever found so far.

**Idea 2 review.** One label in `EvergroveApp.ChoicePreview.cs` now reads `LandmarkCause.For(WorldAfter)` instead of `LocalizedVillage.EnvironmentalRule(WorldAfter)`. `WorldAfter` comes from the real `Choose` on a detached copy, so `Specific` proves the cause from the same conditions the board uses after confirming. `For` falls back to the old rule outside English, for completed gardens and in research modes, so non-English players see no change. The test checks that the line names `GardenCatalog.For(garden, 0, choice).Name` for all three choices, and that making previews leaves the live save byte-identical. PuzzleOnly offers no preview at all, so its check covers `For` on the world itself.

**Idea 3 review.** `SpokenBack` uses the same clamp, wrap, colour name and ordinal as `Spoken`. `TurnToStep` sets `handedBack` only around its own `PaintTurn`, which calls `SpeakTurn` synchronously (`TreePlay.cs`). A `finally` clears the flag. The caption keeps "Back to …" until the next paint. That stays true until someone moves, and the next move repaints the caption.

**Idea 4 result.** Under normal vision, protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia, `LandmarkFrame` on `AnchoredTile` is at least 8.613:1 (deuteranopia), well over 4.5:1. The straw fill differs from dry, wet and solved tiles by at least 0.105 RGB distance (dry tile, tritanopia), over the 0.08 bar. The frame carries the cue for every eye, and the fill is a real second cue. No colour changed.

**Idea 5 review.** `SameLine` is added only in an `else if` after the smaller warning, so the two lines can never both appear. It needs at least one stream on each side, so two fresh gardens still give two lines. The wording names only the four facts compared, because equal counts don't prove two gardens are identical.
