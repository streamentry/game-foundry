**Idea 1 review.** `LinkedForkPlay` keeps its own mask copy; `Tap` turns a cell and its axle partner, `Undo` turns them back three quarters, `Solved` is `ForkBoards.IsComplete`. `FewestTaps` counts each plain cell's fewest clockwise turns plus the axle's shared k once, and throws if no shared k exists. All 12 `DrawByLevel(12, 240)` boards reach `Solved` by those taps, with `Taps == FewestTaps`. Fewest taps by seed: 5→34, 7→30, 0→38, 19→35, 4→41, 128→23, 70→31, 55→33, 51→32, 213→36, 111→39, 3→31. Tap count does not follow the chapter's work curve (board 6 needs 23, board 5 needs 41). Taps measure scramble, not reasoning, so they are not a difficulty measure.

**Idea 2 review.** The old lines produced "A oak, new in the garden", "Someone sent you a olive tree cutting", "A new sunflowers!" and "There's a beehives in your basket". The implementer's plural rule (ends in "s" except "lotus" and "hibiscus") would have made Orin say "Those new vine trellis" and "There are apple press in your basket". The all-items test didn't catch this, because it trusted the same rule. The coordinator changed the rule so names ending in "ss", "us" or "is" count as singular, and pinned both strings. No older test pinned the old wording.

**Idea 3 review.** The line is added only when a together mark resolves to a real item. Grown marks map through `GardenCatalog.For`, the same mapping the almanac uses. Bought marks map through `Nursery.Item`, with range checks. A garden with no marks gets a byte-identical postcard. The test builds its marks through real solves and a restored export, not hand-written JSON. The line names things, never people or counts of people.

**Idea 4 finding.** Eight ink-on-tile pairs were checked under normal vision and the three colour-vision simulations. The finished-stream teal was an inline `Color32(22, 132, 146)` in `PipeGraphic.cs` and measured **2.949:1 on the protected tile under protanopia**. The coordinator moved it into the theme as `MosseloraTheme.SolvedPipe` and darkened it 3% to #158190, which passes 3:1 on every tile it is drawn on. The test now reads the theme value, not a copied literal. The other pairs: dry on dry 4.23, dry on protected 3.80, wet on wet 8.10, wet on protected 6.90, solved on solved 3.61 or above (measured before the change). The hint honey is still an inline literal in `TreePlay.cs`; the test copies it.

**Idea 5 review.** One conditional sentence on the existing match. Tested with a real `SolveTree` then `MarkTogether`, and a byte-identical snapshot check.
