The fifth unattended vision-ideas cycle. Proposal: [vision-ideas-20261128-1201](../specs/proposals/vision-ideas-20261128-1201.md). Designed and reviewed by Opus. ## Ideas and outcome

| # | Idea | Area | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Do bigger forked boards need more reasoning? | Puzzle depth | **Shipped as a hidden gate.** Answer: no more than 5×5 (see below) |
| 2 | The thing's story remembers the table | Truthful memory | **Shipped** |
| 3 | The token nudges whoever is next | Family, one device | **Dropped: it already exists** |
| 4 | Wet pipes read as wet for every eye | Art and readability | **Shipped, with a collar fix** |
| 5 | The spring says where the water must go | Accessibility | **Shipped** |

**Idea 1 result (the cheapest disproof held):** `ForkBoards.Generate(seed, 6)` built a unique 6×6 board for all 40 seeds (0–39), with 7–12 forks. Deduction forced 1,394 of 1,440 cells. 7 of 40 boards (17.5%) needed a guess, at most 4. At 5×5 the share was 14 of 100. A bigger board does not make forks meaningfully deeper. The next depth candidate has to change the rules (for example, a constraint that ties a tile to something other than its neighbours), not the size. The 5×5 boards are byte-identical to before: a fingerprint over seeds 0–99 matched, with the same forced and guess sums (2,409 and 32).

**Why idea 3 was dropped:** the proposal assumed the token only changes colour. In fact `TreePlay.PaintTurn` already pulses the token (`BloomPulse`, the same 0.12 size) on every paint while playing together with motion on. The implementer replaced that with a new coroutine that fires only on a forward move. That isn't clearly better: it would stop signalling a turn handed back by undo. It also added code for no new player value. All of idea 3 was reverted. The family idea for the next run should start from a check of what `TreePlay` already does.

**Idea 4 review fix:** the lightest darkening of `#18717F` that reaches 1.5:1 against `DryChannel` under all four simulations is `#104953` (k = 0.65). The proposal's estimate `#104B54` fails protanopia by 0.002. Measured wet vs dry: normal 1.87, protanopia 1.54, deuteranopia 2.11, tritanopia 1.80. The lowest tile-state contrast is 6.36:1. The implementer left the wet collar at (16,86,98), which is now lighter than the channel and would read as a pale ring at each arm end. The coordinator darkened it by the same ratio to (11,56,64), so the collar sits below the channel as before. `WaterChannel` itself is unchanged, because icons, maps and marks use it. The finished-stream teal is unchanged and stays the brightest state.
