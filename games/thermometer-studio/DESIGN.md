# Thermometer Studio — independent design system (v0.1)

**Positioning:** Fill liquid from each tube's bulb toward its tip only. Row and column totals constrain all eight disjoint two-cell thermometers. Calm puzzle agency, clear mechanical feedback, and no age stereotype, health claim, timer or dark pattern.

**Three adjectives:** laboratory · gentle · balanced.

| Semantic token | Hex | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base | `#F5F8F7` | Warm independent scene background |
| Ink | `#2D4046` | Readable numbers and directions |
| Accent | `#B35166` | Primary controls and focus |
| Soft region | `#E4EEF0` | Secondary controls and puzzle group |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Editable cells or contrast text |

**Typography:** system font, 27–29px headline, 15–17px rule explanations and status, 14–27px puzzle labels, 13px secondary help. **Spacing:** 17–18px horizontal margins, ~12px vertical rhythm, ≥48px grid and action touch targets, ~4px grid gutters. Scroll vertically instead of shrinking critical type.

**Visual focus:** give player a usable first move immediately; non-color text distinguishes errors, clues and progress. Tap a bulb to fill its first segment or a tip to fill both; tap the same target again to empty the tube. Undo and Restart recover mistakes. **Motion:** immediate reversible taps, no countdown, flashing or confetti. **Sound/haptics:** none by default.

**Puzzle feedback:** actual constraint engine, not reference-answer comparison, determines completion. Undo and Restart are visible, preserved entries locally, optional next puzzle after rules verification, and readable messages for conflicts.

**Icon brief:** a short glass thermometer with a rose-colored bulb and two liquid segments. Independent vector illustration, not copied competitor imagery.

**Screenshot brief:** four-by-four tube rack, matched count labels, overflow explanation, finished experiment. Screenshots are planned; no native-phone captures have been completed.

**Accessibility:** large tap targets, focusable controls, relevant cell accessibility labels and non-color explanation. Full nonvisual equivalence, screen-reader navigation, device-safe-area and 200% text scaling **require physical phone testing**. Do not infer age-45+ usability from a desktop Godot scene.

**Originality and validation:** independently authored six boards and controls; GF-021 is an unvalidated idea from the research queue. No claims of proven niche popularity, commercial differentiation, or cognitive treatment.
