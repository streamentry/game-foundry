# I412: Footprints in the snow

Run 3, batch 15.

When snow lies (I338), a trail of small prints runs from the cottage door to each neighbour, left and right in turn: they walked out to meet you. It's wordless and shows only in winter.

- `GardenMapView.Footprints(standing, ...)` runs after the neighbours are placed, with 3 to 24 prints per trail depending on distance, each rotated along the path.
- `FootprintsNow` follows `SeasonLook.SnowLying`.
