# I402: The shady seat

Run 3, batch 13.

The shady seat (something built beside a tree, I380) had no lasting purpose. Now, in the golden hour, the neighbours come and sit by it: Mira to its left, Orin to its right, Fen just below. It's wordless, a little evening ritual the garden makes possible. At dusk they move on to the cottage door (I367).

- `Pairings.SpotFor(..., ShadySeat)` gives the seat's cell.
- `NeighborsOnMap` uses it in the golden hour when the pairing has been found and still stands.
