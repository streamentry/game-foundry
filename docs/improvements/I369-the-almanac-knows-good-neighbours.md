# I369: The almanac knows good neighbours

Run 3, batch 7.

## PM decision

The almanac lists all four pairings. Found ones show in ink with their painting; ones still to find are faint, but their rule is still written. Nothing is a secret: a 40+ player should never have to look things up outside the game. The fun is making the pairing in your own garden, not guessing the rule.

## Dev

- `EvergroveApp.PairingRows(page)` on the almanac page, Living mode only. Each row has a 36 pt painting mark (flower, build, second flower, tree) and the rule text from `Pairings.Hint`.
- Ink when found; faded paper when not.

## Result

A short checklist the player can finish in a few days of play.
