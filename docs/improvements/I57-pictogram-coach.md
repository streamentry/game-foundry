# I57 — Coach bubble becomes a pictogram

Date: 2026-11-24. Branch: feat/mosselora-painted-ui (PR #76).

**Player problem.** Mira's first-stream coach bubble still carried a sentence ("Blue waves = spring · green sprout = garden. Tap to connect water."), the last block of instructional text on the play screen. The brief asks for pictures over words, and the 40–49 band reads pictures fastest.

**Change.** The bubble is now a **pictogram**: the spring-waves glyph in a cream disc → a white arrow → the garden-sprout glyph in a cream disc, then a white **tapping hand** (new Codex glyph from `art/generated/icons-ui-5.png`, together with turn, rooted and sun glyphs for later use). The original sentence stays as transparent text inside the bubble, so screen readers still read it. The bubble still leaves after the first move.

**Evidence.** b36 tour, stream 1 (`I57-I58-pictogram-coach-rooted.png`, left).

**Risk.** A pictogram may teach less than a sentence for some first-time players. This is a first-session (M3) test item: does an uncoached player tap within 15 s?
