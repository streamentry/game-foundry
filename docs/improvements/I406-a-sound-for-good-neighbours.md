# I406: A sound for good neighbours

Run 3, batch 14.

Finding a good neighbour now has its own sound: three soft bell notes rising (C, E, G), slightly apart, instead of the everyday chime. Over time the ear learns that this sound means you discovered something. It's synthesised in `GardenSounds.Neighbours()` (decaying sines with a soft octave), cached, and silent when sound is off.
