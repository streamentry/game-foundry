# I354 — Rainy days

Run 3, batch 4.

## PM decision

On a rainy day:
- rain falls over the garden on a slight slant, calm and steady;
- the light is greyer and cooler;
- the soundscape (I316) is mostly a soft patter.

The neighbours say "Rain today. The garden's drinking it in, and the river's talking louder."

## Dev

- `GardenMapView.WeatherLayer` (Rainy): 70 thin streaks, drawn with motion only, plus a cool wash over the painting under the things.
- `AmbientVoice.Rain`: a synthesised patter of tiny decaying drops under a hush.
- `Soundscape.WeatherVoice` leads the voice 60% of the time.
- Tour shot: `19w-rainy-day` (spring).

## Result

A rainy day feels like one, gently.
