# I424: A hop of joy

Run 3, batch 18.

When you find a good neighbour, the keeper whose line of work it is gives a little hop on the map as you come home, alongside the picture toast and the chime (I387, I406): Mira for a grove or orchard, Orin for something by the water or a shady seat, Fen for the flower pairings. They share the moment without a word. It happens once, and never with reduced motion.

- `celebrateKeeper` is set in `NotePairings`, used by `NeighborsOnMap` (reusing `HelloHop`), and cleared after the hub is built.
