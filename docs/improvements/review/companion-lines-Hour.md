# Companion lines: Hour

**Status: draft, not reviewed.** Written by hand (no script) on 2026-11-27 for smart companion SC04. Nothing here ships until a person has read every line; accepted lines move into `games/evergrove/Assets/Resources/Evergrove/Lines/en.json`, rejected ones are deleted.

**The fact:** the garden's light is on and it is dawn, golden hour, dusk or night (morning and midday say nothing). Anyone may say it.

**Slots:** none. The light picks the list (`Hour.<Phase>`).

**Writing notes:** Nothing that depends on the season or the weather (no fireflies, frost, dew or stars), so every line is true on any day of the year at that hour.

## Mira (voice 0) · Dawn

1. First light on the river. It's the quietest hour there is.
2. Dawn. The trees are waking up slowly, the way old friends do.
3. Early light. My grandmother was always up at this hour, listening to the river.
4. The light's only just come. Everything looks freshly made.
5. Dawn on the water. If you're very still, the whole valley seems to breathe.

## Mira (voice 0) · Golden

1. Golden light through the branches. This is the hour trees look their best.
2. The light's gone gold. My mother called this the honey hour.
3. Long shadows from the trees now. The day's winding down gently.
4. Gold on the water. I like to sit by the river at this hour and do nothing.
5. This light makes the bark look warm, even on the oldest trees.

## Mira (voice 0) · Dusk

1. Dusk. The river goes silver, then grey, then dark.
2. The light's going. The trees turn into shapes against the sky.
3. Dusk. My grandmother said this is when trees tell each other the day's news.
4. Evening's coming down the valley. The river sounds louder at dusk.
5. Dusk. A good time to walk slowly by the water.

## Mira (voice 0) · Night

1. Night in the garden. You can hear the river better in the dark.
2. It's night. The trees are resting, the way we should be.
3. Night on the river. Water sounds different when you can't see it.
4. A quiet night. My mother used to say the trees keep watch while we sleep.
5. Night-time. The garden belongs to the night creatures now.

## Orin (voice 1) · Dawn

1. First light. Best hour for work: cool, quiet, and nobody asking questions.
2. Dawn. The light's low enough to show every crack in the old stones.
3. Early. I've had my tea and I'm ready to start.
4. First light on the lane. Everything still and waiting.
5. Dawn. My father was always at his bench by this hour.

## Orin (voice 1) · Golden

1. Golden light. Wood glows at this hour, every grain of it.
2. The light's gone low and warm. Good time to stand back and look at the day's work.
3. Golden hour. The bridge throws a long shadow over the water.
4. Late gold on the lane. I'm putting my tools away.
5. This light flatters everything, even my old shed.

## Orin (voice 1) · Dusk

1. Dusk. Time to light the lamp by the bridge.
2. The light's going. I'll finish this joint tomorrow.
3. Dusk. Shapes get soft at this hour. Even straight lines look kind.
4. Evening. The lane's quiet and the workshop smells of shavings.
5. Dusk. I like to check the gate latches before dark. Habit.

## Orin (voice 1) · Night

1. Night. The garden looks bigger in the dark.
2. Night-time. I've hung my lantern on the hook by the door.
3. It's late. The tools are away and the shed's locked.
4. Night. You can hear the timbers settle when it goes quiet.
5. A quiet night. Good for thinking, bad for sawing.

## Fen (voice 2) · Dawn

2. First light over the beds. Everything's grey and gentle, waiting for colour.
3. Early light. Spiders have been busy hanging webs between the stems.
4. Dawn. I like to walk the beds before anyone else is about.
5. First light on the flower beds. Everything smells new.

## Fen (voice 2) · Golden

2. Golden light. The whole garden looks like it's glowing.
3. The light's gone gold. The small creatures are finding somewhere to settle.
4. Late gold over the beds. I'd paint this light, if I could paint.
5. Golden hour. My favourite time to just sit and watch.

## Fen (voice 2) · Dusk

2. The light's going. Colours fade first, then shapes.
3. Dusk. The garden goes quiet, one creature at a time.
4. Evening. The beds look softer in this light, like a painting.
5. Dusk. The garden's going blue and soft.

## Fen (voice 2) · Night

1. Night. The flowers are asleep, petals closed tight.
2. Night-time. The garden smells different in the dark. Earthier.
3. It's night. The small creatures have the garden now.
4. Night. Mind the steps by the creek in the dark.
5. A quiet night. Even the bees are dreaming, I expect.
