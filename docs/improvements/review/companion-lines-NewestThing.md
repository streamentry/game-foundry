# Companion lines: NewestThing

**Status: approved by the founder on 2026-11-27 (all 60 lines), and moved into `games/evergrove/Assets/Resources/Evergrove/Lines/en.json`.** Written by hand (no script) for smart companion SC03. This file stays as the review record; `en.json` is what ships.

**The fact:** the newest thing of the speaker's own kind (Mira trees, Orin built things, Fen flowers) and where it stands on the map.

**Slots:** `{thing}`, the item's name (for example "quince tree", "pergola", "cowslips", "lily of the valley"); `{where}`, one of "by the creek", "by the cottage", "down the valley" or "in the meadow".

**Writing notes:** `{thing}` can be plural ("cowslips"), so it is never the subject of a verb. Nothing is claimed about the thing's growth, the weather, the season, the time of day or the player's comings and goings. Colour comes from the neighbour's own habits and sayings.
