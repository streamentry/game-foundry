# Companion lines: RiverFurther

**Status: draft, not reviewed.** Written by hand (no script) on 2026-11-27 for smart companion SC04. Nothing here ships until a person has read every line; accepted lines move into `games/evergrove/Assets/Resources/Evergrove/Lines/en.json`, rejected ones are deleted.

**The fact:** tending has just opened new river past the story's end, and the new stretch brought a season. Mira says it.

**Slots:** none. The season picks the list (`RiverFurther.<Season>`).

**Writing notes:** Each line names its own season, so it is always true. Nothing about what's in the new stretch beyond its season.

## Mira (voice 0) · Spring

1. The river runs further than it ever has, and spring came down with it. The new bank is all blossom.
2. New river, and it brought spring. The new bank is soft and green.
3. The river's opened further, and the new stretch is pure spring. Buds on everything.
4. Further than ever, the river runs now. Spring came with the new water.
5. There's new river down the valley, and it's spring there. My grandmother loved the first green of a new bank.

## Mira (voice 0) · Summer

1. The river runs further than it ever has. Summer came with it, warm and green.
2. New river, and it's summer on the new bank. The water's warm enough for paddling.
3. The river's opened down the valley. It brought summer: long grass, slow water.
4. Further than ever now. The new stretch came with summer, and the dragonflies found it first.
5. There's new river, and summer with it. The new bank's just waiting for roots.

## Mira (voice 0) · Autumn

2. New river, and it brought autumn. Leaves are riding the water down.
3. The river's opened further. The new stretch is all autumn, gold and quiet.
4. Further than it ever ran, the river. Autumn came down with it, red and gold.
5. There's new river, and the new bank is autumn-coloured. My father loved this kind of light.

## Mira (voice 0) · Winter

1. The river runs further than ever, and winter came with it. Even the new bank is frosted.
2. New river, and it brought winter. The new bank is white and quiet.
3. The river's opened further. Winter came with it; the new stretch sparkles with frost.
4. Further than ever now. The new bank is wintry, and the water's clear as glass.
5. There's new river under the frost. Come spring, it'll want roots.
