# Companion lines: LetterWaiting

**Status: approved by the founder on 2026-11-27 (every line), and moved into `games/evergrove/Assets/Resources/Evergrove/Lines/en.json`.** Written by hand (no script) for smart companion SC04. This file stays as the review record; `en.json` is what ships.

**The fact:** a letter or note is in your letterbox, unread on this device. Orin keeps an eye on the lane, so only he says it.

**Slots:** `{name}`, who it's from: a family member's name (up to 24 characters), or Mira, Orin or Fen for the neighbours' own notes.

**Writing notes:** It may wait as long as it likes: nothing presses you to read it. Orin never claims to have opened or read it. One known quirk, as before: when the note is Orin's own, he says it's from Orin.

## Orin (voice 1)

1. There's a letter in your box from {name}. I saw the flag up as I passed.
2. Letter for you, from {name}. It's in your box, waiting.
3. Your letterbox has something in it: a letter from {name}.
4. I noticed the flag on your letterbox. A letter from {name}, by the look of it.
5. There's post for you. A letter from {name}, in your box by the gate.
6. A letter from {name} is waiting in your box.
7. You've a letter in your box, from {name}. Letters keep; take your time.
8. I don't read other people's post, but I do notice flags. There's one up for {name}'s letter.
9. Something from {name} in your letterbox. I'd read it sitting down, with a cup of tea.
10. A letter from {name} for you. A letter is worth two of anything else, I always say.
11. Your box has a letter from {name} in it. I gave the hinge a drop of oil while I was there.
12. The flag's up on your letterbox. A letter from {name}.
13. There's a letter from {name} waiting in your box. It'll keep as long as you like.
14. Post for you: one letter, from {name}.
15. I straightened your letterbox flag. It's up for a letter from {name}.
16. A letter from {name} came. It's in your box, safe and dry.
17. There's a letter from {name} in your box. Somebody's thinking of you.
18. You'll find a letter from {name} in your letterbox, whenever you're ready.
19. A letter's arrived from {name}. Your box has it, safe.
20. Letters are good things. There's one from {name} in your box.
