# Companion lines: SeasonTurned

**Status: draft, not reviewed.** Written by hand (no script) on 2026-11-27 for smart companion SC04. Nothing here ships until a person has read every line; accepted lines move into `games/evergrove/Assets/Resources/Evergrove/Lines/en.json`, rejected ones are deleted.

**The fact:** the garden is in a new season (never its first one), said once per season turn by one neighbour.

**Slots:** none. The season picks the list (`SeasonTurned.<Season>`).

**Writing notes:** Short, plain news in each voice. The season's letter (SeasonTales) still arrives by post as before; these lines don't repeat its stories.

## Mira (voice 0) · Spring

1. Spring's come to the garden. The trees are thinking about leaves.
2. It's spring. The sap's rising; you can almost hear it.
3. Spring has turned the corner into the garden. Everything's waking slowly.

## Mira (voice 0) · Summer

1. Summer's here, and the trees have their full shade.
2. It's summer now. The river's slow and warm.
3. Summer in the garden. Long days, long shadows, and the trees at their fullest.

## Mira (voice 0) · Autumn

1. Autumn's come. The leaves are thinking about gold.
2. It's autumn now. My father always said autumn is the trees letting go gracefully.
3. Autumn in the garden. The river carries the first leaves down.

## Mira (voice 0) · Winter

1. Winter's here. The trees are resting, and they've earned it.
2. It's winter now. Bare branches show you the shape of a tree.
3. Winter in the garden. The river runs clear and cold.

## Orin (voice 1) · Spring

1. Spring's here. Time to check what the winter loosened.
2. It's spring now. The lane's soft underfoot.
3. Spring. I'll be out mending fences while the ground's kind.

## Orin (voice 1) · Summer

1. Summer's come. Long days for long jobs.
2. It's summer now. Wood dries fast; good for building.
3. Summer. The bridge stones are warm by noon.

## Orin (voice 1) · Autumn

1. Autumn's here. I'll be sweeping leaves off the bridge till winter.
2. It's autumn now. Good weather for oiling hinges and stacking wood.
3. Autumn. The lane's full of leaves and the shed smells of woodsmoke.

## Orin (voice 1) · Winter

1. Winter's come. I've got the stove going in the workshop.
2. It's winter now. Frost shows you which joints were done right.
3. Winter. I'll be gritting the bridge on cold mornings.

## Fen (voice 2) · Spring

1. Spring's here. The first shoots are up and the bees are waking.
2. It's spring now. I've been counting buds; I've lost count.
3. Spring. Every bed's full of little green beginnings.

## Fen (voice 2) · Summer

1. Summer's here. The flowers are wide open and the butterflies are out.
2. It's summer now. The bees are busier than I've ever been.
3. Summer in the garden. Everything's buzzing.

## Fen (voice 2) · Autumn

1. Autumn's come. Time to save seeds for next year.
2. It's autumn now. The last flowers are the bravest.
3. Autumn. The spiders are spinning big webs between the stems.

## Fen (voice 2) · Winter

1. Winter's here. The beds are resting under their blankets.
2. It's winter now. The hedgehogs are asleep and the snowdrops are thinking.
3. Winter. I'm feeding the birds and dreaming about spring.
