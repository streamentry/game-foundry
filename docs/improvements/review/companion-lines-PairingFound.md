# Companion lines: PairingFound

**Status: draft, not reviewed.** Written by hand (no script) on 2026-11-27 for smart companion SC04. Nothing here ships until a person has read every line; accepted lines move into `games/evergrove/Assets/Resources/Evergrove/Lines/en.json`, rejected ones are deleted.

**The fact:** a good-neighbour pairing of the speaker's kind has been found in your garden. Said once.

**Slots:** none. The pairing picks the list (`PairingFound.<Pairing>`).

**Writing notes:** Each line names what was found, by its real rule. Mentions of a visitor or a yield say "may" or "come autumn": those still depend on the hour and season.

## Mira (voice 0) · Grove

1. You've found a grove: two trees side by side. They'll keep each other company.
2. A grove in your garden now. My grandmother said trees are happiest in pairs.
3. Two trees together: you've made a grove. I'm so pleased.
4. Your grove is found. An owl may come to see it, after dark.
