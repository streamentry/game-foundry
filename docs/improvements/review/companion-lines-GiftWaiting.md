**Status: approved by the founder on 2026-11-27 (all 60 lines), and moved into `games/evergrove/Assets/Resources/Evergrove/Lines/en.json`.** Written by hand (no script) for smart companion SC03. This file stays as the review record; `en.json` is what ships.

**The fact:** a plant of the speaker's kind is waiting in your basket to be placed. It is a "waiting" fact, so it may be mentioned on every visit until placed. That is why it needs many wordings.

**Slots:** `{thing}`, the nursery item's name (for example "apple tree", "woodworking shed", "lily of the valley").

**Writing notes:**

- The basket holds cuttings from family parcels, neighbours' cuttings and keepsakes. The save doesn't record which, so no line says who sent it.
- Orin's things are built things, so his lines never say "cutting".
- Never "a {thing}" (an article clash), and `{thing}` is never a verb's subject.
- No hurry and no "still": it can wait as long as it likes.
- Lines that mention placing it near a tree, by the water, or beside another flower follow the garden's real good-neighbour rules.
