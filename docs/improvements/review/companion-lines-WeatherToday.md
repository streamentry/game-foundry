# Companion lines: WeatherToday

**Status: approved by the founder on 2026-11-27 (every line), and moved into `games/evergrove/Assets/Resources/Evergrove/Lines/en.json`.** Written by hand (no script) for smart companion SC04. This file stays as the review record; `en.json` is what ships.

**The fact:** today's weather in the garden is rainy, windy, hot or snowy (fair days say nothing). Anyone may say it; the ranker gives it to one neighbour with nothing better.

**Slots:** none. The weather picks the list (`WeatherToday.<Weather>`).

**Writing notes:** Each voice keeps to its own line of work: Mira trees and river, Orin roofs, bridge and tools, Fen flowers and small creatures. No claims about the season beyond what the weather implies.

## Mira (voice 0) · Rainy

1. Rain today. The trees are standing very still and drinking.
2. Rain on the river today. It makes the water sound like it's whispering.
3. A rainy one. My grandmother said rain is the trees' favourite song.
4. Rain today. The bark goes dark and the moss goes bright. Trees look their best wet.
5. It's raining. A good day to stand under a tree and listen.

## Mira (voice 0) · Windy

1. Windy today. The branches are talking to each other.
2. A windy one. Listen to the trees; they sound like the sea.
3. The wind's up today. Young trees bend so they don't break. Wise of them.
4. Windy today. My mother hung washing in this sort of wind; dry in an hour.
5. The wind's in the branches today. Every tree has its own voice in a wind.

## Mira (voice 0) · Hot

1. Hot today. The best place in the garden is under a tree, and the trees know it.
2. A hot one. The river's slow and lazy, and so am I.
3. Hot today. Trees give shade without being asked. I've always loved that about them.
4. A warm day. The leaves turn their edges to the sun, to keep cool.
5. Hot today. Sit in the shade a while, if you like. The trees won't mind.

## Mira (voice 0) · Snowy

1. Snow today. The trees are wearing white and standing very proud.
2. Snowing. The river's gone quiet under the snow, the way a house goes quiet when everyone's asleep.
3. Snow on the branches today. My grandmother said snow is a blanket the sky lends the trees.
4. A snowy day. Every branch has a white line along it, neat as stitching.
5. Snow today. The trees don't mind; they've been through more winters than any of us.

## Orin (voice 1) · Rainy

1. Rain today. I've cleared the drains on the bridge. Water likes a way out.
2. Raining. Good day for the workshop; I'll mend what needs mending.
3. Rain today. Wood darkens when it's wet, and you can see the grain properly.
4. A wet one. The lane's puddled, but the bridge stones are holding fine.
5. Rain today. A roof shows its worth in weather like this.

## Orin (voice 1) · Windy

1. Windy today. I've tied down anything that might wander off.
2. A windy one. You learn which joints are sound on a day like this.
3. The wind's up. I checked the shed door; it's latched.
4. Windy today. Good drying weather for timber.
5. A blustery day. Everything's creaking, but nothing's moving that shouldn't.

## Orin (voice 1) · Hot

1. Hot today. Wood swells in the heat and doors stick. I'll leave the shed open.
2. A hot one. I've put my tools in the shade. Metal gets too warm to hold.
3. Hot today. Good weather for paint to dry. Not for the painter, mind.
4. Hot today. The water under the bridge is the coolest thing in the valley.
5. A scorcher. I'll do my sawing early and my sitting late.

## Orin (voice 1) · Snowy

1. Snow today. I've swept the path so nobody slips.
2. Snowing. Snow shows you every footstep. I can see where the birds have been.
3. Snow today. A good roof holds it; a poor one tells you soon enough.
4. A snowy day. I've got the stove lit in the workshop. Warm your hands there if you like.
5. Snow on the rails today. I'll put a bit of grit on the bridge.

## Fen (voice 2) · Rainy

1. Rain today. The soil's drinking deep; the roots will be glad of it.
2. Raining. Worms come up to feel the rain, and the blackbirds are very pleased about that.
3. Rain today. Puddles in the paths, and the beds are dark and soft.
4. A rainy one. Raindrops sit on every stem like tiny glass beads.
5. Rain today. The bees are staying home. Sensible bees.

## Fen (voice 2) · Windy

2. A windy one. The flowers are dancing whether they want to or not.
3. Windy today. I've staked the tallest stems so they don't snap.
4. The wind's up. Everything small is tucked away somewhere snug.
5. Windy today. Leaves and bits of straw blowing about like confetti.

## Fen (voice 2) · Hot

1. Hot today. The bees are drinking at the edge of the water, little sips.
2. A hot one. The flowers smell strongest in the heat. Breathe in.
3. Hot today. The butterflies love it. They open their wings to soak up the sun.
4. Hot today. I've filled a saucer with water for the bees, with pebbles so they don't fall in.
5. A warm day. The ladybirds are out sunning themselves.

## Fen (voice 2) · Snowy

1. Snow today. The hedgehogs are fast asleep, and quite right too.
2. Snowing. Under the snow, the bulbs are waiting. They know what they're doing.
3. Snow today. I scattered seed for the birds by my gate.
4. Robins follow gardeners in the snow, hoping for a worm. Mine's been at my heels.
5. Snow today. Ladybirds sleep through it, tucked together in a crack in the bark.
