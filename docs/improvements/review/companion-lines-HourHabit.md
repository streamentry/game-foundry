# Companion lines: HourHabit

**Status: draft, not reviewed.** Written by hand (no script) on 2026-11-27 for smart companion SC04. Nothing here ships until a person has read every line; accepted lines move into `games/evergrove/Assets/Resources/Evergrove/Lines/en.json`, rejected ones are deleted.

**The fact:** from this device's own count of when you come, one hour of the day is clearly yours (at least six returns, most of them at that hour). Only the neighbour who'd notice that hour says it: Mira dawn and morning, Orin golden hour and dusk, Fen midday and night.

**Slots:** none. The hour picks the list (`HourHabit.<Phase>`).

**Writing notes:** An observation, never an expectation. No "I'll be waiting" or "I look out for you", nothing about coming at other times, and no "again" or "every day". "Often" and "usually" are true; "always" isn't.

## Mira (voice 0) · Dawn

1. You often come at first light. It's the garden's quietest hour; you've chosen well.
2. Early riser, I think. You and the river are up before the rest of us.
3. You like the garden at dawn. So did my grandmother; she said it's when the day's still deciding.
4. You come early, I've noticed. The trees are at their stillest then.
5. Dawn suits you. The light's soft and nobody's in a hurry.
6. You're often here at first light. That's when the river sounds most like itself.
7. I've noticed you like the dawn. There's no better hour for thinking.
8. An early visitor. The garden's glad of company before the bees are up.
9. You often come with the first light. My father did his best work at that hour.
10. You like it early. I do too. The day feels new and nobody's used it yet.

## Mira (voice 0) · Morning

1. Mornings suit you. You catch the garden while it's fresh.
2. You often come in the morning. That's when the trees stretch, I always think.
3. A morning visitor. My mother said mornings are for gardens and afternoons for people.
4. I've noticed you come in the mornings. The light's kind then.
5. You like the morning garden. So do the birds.
6. Morning seems to be your hour. Everything's still a little sleepy and soft.
7. You're often here in the morning. That's when the river's at its clearest.
8. Mornings, mostly, for you. A good habit, my grandmother would say.
9. You come in the mornings, I've noticed. The garden gets the best of you.
10. You like a morning visit. There's a freshness then that doesn't last.

## Orin (voice 1) · Golden

1. You often come as the light goes gold. Best light for looking at work, I always say.
2. Golden hour seems to be yours. It's when the stones look their best.
3. You like the late gold light, I've noticed. So do I. It makes everything look finished.
4. You're often here as the sun gets low. A good time to look back on the day.
5. Golden hour, mostly, for you. My father called it the builder's reward.
6. I've noticed you come in the late light. It flatters the garden, and the garden deserves it.
7. You like the golden hour. Long shadows, warm stone. Can't beat it.
8. As the light goes gold, that's when I tend to see you about.
9. You come late in the day, in the gold. The bridge looks its best then.
10. Golden light seems to suit you. It suits the garden too.

## Orin (voice 1) · Dusk

1. You often come at dusk. I light the lamp by the bridge about then.
2. Dusk is your hour, I've noticed. The day's work is done and the garden's quiet.
3. You like the garden at dusk. It's a good time to look at things without fussing.
4. You come in the evening, mostly. A sensible hour; the day's done its worst.
5. A dusk visitor. My father liked to walk the lane at this hour, checking the gates.
6. I've noticed you come as the light goes. The garden looks softer then.
7. Evenings seem to be yours. The workshop smells of shavings and it's all put away.
8. You often come at dusk. That's when the river goes grey and calm.
9. You like the evening garden. So do I; it's the best hour for sitting.
10. Dusk suits you. I always think the garden's at its friendliest then.

## Fen (voice 2) · Day

1. You like the garden in the full of the day, when everything can be seen properly.
2. You often come in the middle of the day, when the light's at its best.

7. You're often here when the sun's high. The flowers love an audience.

## Fen (voice 2) · Night

1. You often come at night. The flowers are asleep, but the garden's still here for you.
2. A night visitor. The night creatures will be glad of the company.
3. You like the garden at night, I've noticed. It smells different in the dark.
4. Night seems to be your hour. Quiet, and nobody needs anything.
5. You come late, mostly. The garden's restful then.
6. You're often here after dark. Mind the steps by the creek.
7. A night-time gardener. My grandmother said those are the dreamers.
8. I've noticed you come at night. The garden's a different place then; gentler.
9. You like the night garden. The flowers keep their secrets better in the dark.
10. Night visits, mostly, for you. The petals are closed, but the garden's glad of you.
