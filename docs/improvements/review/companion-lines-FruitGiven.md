# Companion lines: FruitGiven

**Status: draft, not reviewed.** Written by hand (no script) on 2026-11-27 for smart companion SC03. Nothing here ships until a person has read every line; accepted lines move into `games/evergrove/Assets/Resources/Evergrove/Lines/en.json`, rejected ones are deleted.

**The fact:** you gave this neighbour fruit (the newest gift went to them). Said straight away as a thank-you, and later as a remembered kindness.

**Slots:** none. The save doesn't record which fruit or how much, so no line names a fruit or a number.

**Writing notes:** no line asks for more fruit, counts gifts, or makes the thank-you depend on coming back.

## Mira (voice 0)

1. Thank you for the fruit. I'll dry some for the winter, the way my mother did.
2. Fruit from your garden. I'll eat it slowly, by the window, which is the best way.
3. My grandmother said fruit given tastes better than fruit picked. She was right.
4. Thank you. I'll keep a little back for the birds. They helped, in their way.
5. You shouldn't have. But I'm glad you did.
6. I'll make a compote and think of your trees with every spoonful.
7. Thank you for the fruit. There's a bowl on my table that was waiting for something like this.
8. Fruit from a neighbour's tree is the sweetest kind. Thank you.
9. I'll save the stones and see if one takes. Then there'll be a little of your garden in mine.
10. That's kind of you. I'll share it round, if you don't mind.
11. Thank you. My mother always said never hand back an empty basket. I'll think of something.
12. Lovely. I'll have some now and some later, and the later ones will taste of today.
13. You've a generous hand. I'll put this somewhere cool.
14. Oh, thank you. I'll bake with it, and there'll be a slice put by for you.
15. My grandmother wrapped good fruit in paper to keep. I'll do the same with this.
16. Thank you. Some gifts are small and some are just right. This is just right.
17. Thank you. I'll sit under my own tree and eat it, which feels like the proper thing.
18. That's very good of you. I'll put it on the sill where I can see it.
19. Thank you, truly. A bit of fruit and a bit of kindness go a long way together.
20. Fruit from your trees, given by your hand. That's two good things at once. Thank you.
