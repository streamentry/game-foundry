# I391: Autumn's orchard, winter's jam

Run 3, batch 11.

- **Autumn:** an orchard (a tree touching two others, one of the deeper pairings, I380) is ready to pick: 5 fruit, once that autumn. This gives the deeper pairings a lasting purpose.
- **Winter:** nothing grows, so it's the neighbours' turn. If you gave any of them fruit through the year (I228), the one you gave to last brings you a jar of their jam. The toast shows their portrait and the jar. It's reciprocity, which is how neighbours actually are, and it rewards the sharing loop.

Both respect the fruit and jam caps.
