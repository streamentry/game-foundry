# I366 — Dew and frost

Run 3, batch 6.

## PM decision

At dawn and in the morning, dew glints on the grass, twinkling with motion on. In winter it's frost: still, whiter glints. Gone by day, and not at night.

## Dev

- `DayLight.Dew(phase, season)` (Core, new `Dew` enum): Dew, Frost or None. Tested.
- `GardenMapView` scatters up to 36 soft glints over land (fixed seed), with `Twinkle` for dew.
- The tour's dawn shot (`20-light-06`) shows it.

## Result

Mornings have their own look.
