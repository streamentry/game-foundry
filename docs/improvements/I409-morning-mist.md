# I409: Morning mist

Run 3, batch 15.

At dawn and in the morning, soft banks of mist lie low on the river: up to 16, spread along the water, breathing slowly when motion is on. They burn off by day, and don't form on hot or windy days. Early risers see a different garden.

- `DayLight.Mist(phase, weather)`, tested.
- `GardenMapView.MistNow`: SoftGlow ellipses on a spread of water cells.
