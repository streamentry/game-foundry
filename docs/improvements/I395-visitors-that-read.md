## Player note (P21, I383 tour shot on b320)

On a zoomed-out map, the heron rendered at about half size. Its thin neck and beak vanished, leaving a grey "umbrella". The kingfisher was barely visible.

## Fix

- Creatures now scale with the map's cells (`cellW / 30`, clamped to 1–2.4), so they keep their size relative to the trees.
- The heron is redrawn bolder: longer legs in a ripple, a body tipped back, a thicker S of neck, a bigger head with a dark crest and an eye, and a long yellow bill.
