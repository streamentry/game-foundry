# I408: The gift idea, remembered

Run 3, batch 15 ("a place that's lived in").

The visit's gift idea (I398) now carries through to sending. When you open the family sheet, cuttings of the suggested kind come first in the gift step, each with a small sprig badge. Seeing the idea and sending it takes one tap, with no menu to search.

- `FamilySheet.IdeaFamily` is set by `VisitGiftIdea` on each visit and cleared when there's no idea.
- The gift step sorts owned cuttings so matching ones come first, and adds a sprig ornament to them.
