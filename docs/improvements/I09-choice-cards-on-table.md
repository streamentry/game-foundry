# I09 — Choices become three painted cards side by side

Date: 2026-11-24. Branch: feat/mosselora-painted-ui (PR #76).

**Player problem.** The three village choices were stacked full-width rows of text: a web form at the game's most meaningful moment.

**Change.** The choices are now three tall painted cards side by side, like cards laid on a table. Each card has the Codex watercolor-paper body, an 84 pt painted picture (tree, bridge, wildflowers), the choice name in bold and its consequence beneath. The cards share one height, press with the tactile squash, and none is preselected (research neutrality kept). Button names (`Choice ShadeTree` etc.) and spoken labels are unchanged.
