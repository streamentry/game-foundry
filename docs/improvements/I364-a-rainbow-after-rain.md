# I364 — A rainbow after rain

Run 3, batch 6.

## PM decision

On the first fair day after a rainy one (I353), a rainbow arcs softly over the top of home, by day only. It's a small reward for coming back after a rainy day, and it costs nothing: nobody is penalised for missing it.

## Dev

- `DayWeather.Rainbow(day, season, garden)` (Core): today is fair and yesterday rained. Tested over 200 days: exactly then, and it happens.
- `MosseloraArt.Halo()` is a thin soft circle. `GardenMapView` stacks six translucent colour bands (30%) in a masked strip, so only the arc shows.
- Tour shot: `19r-rainbow`.

## Result

One more reason to look in after a grey day.
