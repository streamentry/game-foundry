# I17 — Title becomes the village hub: your creek and neighbors

Date: 2026-11-24. Branch: feat/mosselora-painted-ui (PR #76).

**Player problem (I12 finding 2).** The opening screen was a generic painting, the same for everyone. The strongest non-manipulative reason to come back daily is *your own place*: the garden as you left it, and the people in it.

**Change.** The title screen is now the **village hub**:
- **Top:** the village painting, clipped to the upper half and melting into parchment.
- **Middle:** **your creek** (`PaintedVillage`), with its real water state and your planted trees, crossings and wildflowers. Mira, Orin and Fen stand in it.
- **Bottom:** the wordmark, tagline, your stepping stones and one big play button.
- **Neighbors:** tapping one steps straight from the hub into their memory card. The page reveals the card wherever it sits; that works from the page too.

**Vision fit.** §4.2 "a garden left alone waits unchanged" and §4.3 "neighbors who remember". The reason to return is the place itself, never a streak or a timer.

**Evidence.** b22 launch on the iOS Simulator (`I16-I17-hub-coach.png`, left): a fresh garden shows the dry creek with all three neighbors. The QA tour produced 17 screens.
