# I37 — Choice cards show who will remember

Date: 2026-11-24. Branch: feat/mosselora-painted-ui (PR #76).

**Player problem.** The three choices looked like abstract options. The point of choosing is that *someone* will remember it, and the cards did not say who.

**Change.** Each choice card's painting carries a small portrait of the neighbor who will keep that memory (tree → Mira, crossing → Orin, wildflowers → Fen), pinned to its corner, matching the scrapbook pages (I18). The choice becomes a gift for a person.

**Vision fit.** §4.3: neighbors remember, and the player can see in advance whose memory they are shaping.

**Evidence.** b30 tour (`I36-I37-cards-quiet-restart.png`, left).
