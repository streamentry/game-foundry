# I327 — Who visits, and why

Idea 19 of the [fifty](../../games/evergrove/docs/specs/proposals/world-games-50-ideas-2026-11-27.md), after the joy of a full page in Japanese creature-collecting games, without the hunt.

## Player

"Why do the bees come to my sister's garden and not mine?"

## PM decision

The almanac opens with a visitors section under the memory shelf. Bees, birds and butterflies each have a colour mark and one true line saying what brings them: "Bees come once three flowers are growing, on warm days." Those who've come are in ink; the rest are pencilled. It's a page of reasons, not a checklist with rewards.

## Dev

- `VisitorsRows` in the almanac reads `Visitors.Welcomes` and `Visitors.Why` (tested in I325).

## Result

Visitors are explained, so a player can choose to welcome them. The marks are plain colour discs until painted visitor art exists.
