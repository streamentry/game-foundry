# I48 — The stone just passed lights up on arrival

Date: 2026-11-24. Branch: feat/mosselora-painted-ui (PR #76).

**Player problem.** Moving on to the next stream advanced the stepping stones with no moment of arrival.

**Change.** On the first view of a new stream, the stone just passed **lights up once**: a 45% swell over 0.36 s after a 0.25 s beat, alongside the tiles settling in (I39). `BloomPulse` gained an optional amplitude for small elements. It plays once per stream and only with gentle motion on.

**Evidence.** b34 QA tour, 19 screens. **Not verified:** frame by frame.
