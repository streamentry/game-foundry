# I365 — Today's weather at the cottage

Run 3, batch 6.

## PM decision

The cottage card (I299) names today's weather under the season, with a mark:
- a drop for rain;
- the frosted stone for snow;
- the leaf-blown stone for wind;
- the sun for fair and hot days.

It shows only when the light follows your day.

## Dev

- `OpenCottage` adds a `CottageLine` from `TodayWeather`, using painted marks so nothing white vanishes on the paper.

## Result

The day's weather is stated as well as shown.
