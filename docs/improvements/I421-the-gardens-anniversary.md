# I421: The garden's anniversary

Run 3, batch 17.

On the day a year after the garden began (from the first day it was opened on this device), Orin keeps a note in the letterbox: "A year today since this garden began. We were saying, the three of us, how different the river looks now. Thank you for coming back to it." It's once that day, every year, counting the years. A leap-day garden keeps its day on 28 February.

- `Companion.Anniversary(first, today)` and `AnniversaryNote(years)`.
- Device-only keys: `evergrove:first-seen:<garden>` and `evergrove:anniversary:<garden>` (last year noted).
- Tested: one and two years, not the day after, not the first day, leap day.
