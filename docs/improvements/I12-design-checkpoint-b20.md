# I12 — Haiku art-director checkpoint on b20 sets the next iterations

Date: 2026-11-24. Branch: feat/mosselora-painted-ui (PR #76).

**What.** A design checkpoint: a fresh Haiku sub-agent, acting as art director, reviewed eight b20 iOS screenshots (title, stream 1, choice, memory, stream 2, journal, ending, menu). The brief was immersive rather than web-like, smart navigation, icon-first, and daily appeal for players 40+ without manipulation.

**Scores (1–10).** Immersion 5 · Navigation 5 · Icon-first 4 · Visual polish 6 · "Would open daily" 5.

**Findings, ranked, and the iteration that takes each on:**
1. Stream screens: the teaching line above the board and the caption below it read like a page. Replace them with a one-time coach mark. **→ I16**
2. Make the village itself the home hub: your own creek, neighbors, journal and menu in one painted scene, instead of a generic title painting. This gives a non-manipulative reason to return (your garden, as you left it). **→ I17**
3. Choice screen: the solved line "The water flows through every tile" is duplicated. **→ I14**
4. Memory card: "Mira remembers the tree you planted" appears twice. Show the neighbor's portrait and one sentence. **→ I14**
5. The journal list should become a painted scrapbook, one picture per choice. **→ later iteration**
6. Undo / Guide me / Restart still carry words. The menu's icons have no names. Words belong in the settings page, not the play bar. **→ I15 (menu labels), I16 (tool captions)**

**Disagreement recorded.** The play bar keeps its stream-1 captions only until the coach mark lands (I16). Removing them earlier would trade learnability for purity, which risks the M3 gate (≥80% explain the goal unaided).
