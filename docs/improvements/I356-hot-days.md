# I356 — Hot days

Run 3, batch 4.

## PM decision

On a hot summer day:
- the light is warmer;
- heat shimmers up in slow, clear bands;
- the hum of insects is heard by day;
- the breeze drops, and things barely sway.

"A hot one today. The bees are slow and the shade is busy."
