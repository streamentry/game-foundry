# I425: Moonlight on the water

Run 3, batch 18.

On a clear night (not rain, not snow), a few pale-blue glints twinkle slowly on the river: moonlight. It goes with the owl (I376), the fireflies (I279) and the lamp (I296) to make the night garden worth opening.

- `RiverMoments.Moonlight(phase, weather)`, tested.
- `RiverLifeLayer.Moon` gives fewer, paler, slower sparkles than the golden glitter (I415).
