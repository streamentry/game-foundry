# I415: Golden glitter

Run 3, batch 16.

In the golden hour of a clear day (fair, hot or windy), the river glitters: 14 warm sparkles twinkle across the water. With reduced motion they stay still.

- `RiverMoments.Glitter(season, phase, weather)`, tested: not on rainy days, not outside the golden hour, not on a winter river.
