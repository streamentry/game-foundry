# I341 — Summer you can see

Run 3, batch 1.

## Player

"Summer is just the garden. Nothing says it's summer."

## PM decision

Summer days have dappled sunlight: soft warm patches wandering slowly over the garden, as through leaves. Bees and butterflies are out (I325), and evenings bring fireflies (I279). Summer stays the still season: no drift.

## Dev

- `GardenMapView.SunDapple`: seven soft warm glows (16% strength), drifting a few pixels a second.
  - Only by day in summer, only with motion; nothing at dusk or night.

## Result

Summer has warmth you can see, without clutter.
