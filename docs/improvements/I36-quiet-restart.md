# I36 — Restart becomes the quietest play button

Date: 2026-11-24. Branch: feat/mosselora-painted-ui (PR #76).

**Player problem.** Restart stream was the loudest button on the play bar (terracotta), louder than Undo and Guide me, though it is the one action that throws work away. Good game UI makes the destructive action the quietest.

**Change.** Restart now uses the quiet parchment tone with an ink icon and a drawn edge, while Undo (forest) and Guide me (teal) stay the coloured, inviting actions. Its position and the 0.45 s press-and-hold name are unchanged.

**Evidence.** b30 tour, stream 2 (`I36-I37-cards-quiet-restart.png`, right).
