# I69 — Stones ahead show the seasons to come

Date: 2026-11-24. Branch: feat/mosselora-painted-ui (PR #76).

**Player problem (I67 review: daily appeal 5/10).** The reason to come back has to be honest: no streak, no timer, no "come back tomorrow" (vision §2.6). Players could not see the journey ahead; the stones ahead were all the same faint grey.

**Change.** The stepping stones ahead (hub and stream pages) wear **the season they will be walked in**: summer sage for streams 1–6, autumn amber for 7–9, winter frost for 10–12, matching the seasonal villages from I35. Passed stones stay teal, and the current stream is the leaf-green ring. The year ahead is visible, and it never moves by the clock: it waits until the player walks there.

**Evidence.** b45 launch hub (`I69-seasonal-stones.png`): ring on stone 1, then five sage, three amber and three frost stones above "First Bend". QA tour: 19 screens.
