# I205 — The depth spine: one loop, many layers

## Player
- After I204 a thing holds a puzzle and pays seeds, but seeds buy nothing yet,
  every puzzle is the same kind, neighbours don't notice what I do, and there's
  no way to share my garden with family beyond a file copy. The pieces are good
  but don't add up yet.

## PM decision
- The founder's goal (2026-11-26) asks for easy play with deep, logical
  systems, a simple economy, family visits and gifts, a subtle AI companion and
  a river that can grow forever. Before building more pieces, write one spine
  that every next iteration must fit:
  `games/evergrove/docs/specs/proposals/depth-spine-2026-11-26.md`.
- The loop: restore water → grow → it holds a puzzle → seeds → the nursery →
  new things with new puzzles → the river grows.
- Puzzle kind follows family (water for trees, pairs for flowers, mosaic for
  buildings): variety without menus, and logical.
- The companion starts as neighbours who notice true facts from your save,
  offline. A real language-model companion and the family backend are founder
  decisions (privacy, cost, child co-players).
- Family starts with no server: visit a garden file, send a parcel (seeds, a
  cutting, a letter) through any messaging app.

## Dev
- Design document only; no code change.

## Result
- Next iterations ordered: nursery, pairs, mosaic, noticing neighbours,
  bloom polish, family parcel.
