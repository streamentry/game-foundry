# I56 — Primary audience widened to adults 40+

Date: 2026-11-24. Branch: feat/mosselora-painted-ui (PR #76).

**Direction.** The founder changed the target audience from **45+ to 40+** (2026-11-24).

**Change.** The governing documents now say 40+:
- Root `AGENTS.md`: mission, audience section, preflight table, work cycle, invariants, roles and definition of done.
- Mosselora's `vision.md`, `design.md`, `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`, `README.md` and handbook (`docs/README.md`, `PLAYER_EXPERIENCE.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, `DECISIONS_AND_RISKS.md`, `VALIDATION.md`, Vietnamese summary).
- Specs 01 and 07.
- Test bands become **40–49, 50–64, 65+**.
- A decision-log entry records the change and its limits.

**What this means for the design work.** Nothing in the UI targets age, and none should. The I01–I55 decisions were made for readability, calm and agency (large targets, adjustable text and board, gentle motion, no timers), and they hold for 40-somethings as well. The new 40–49 band adds one risk to watch: some players may find the coach bubble and hint steps too explicit. The press-and-hold names and icon-only tools (I53) already suit faster learners.

**Not changed (on purpose).** Research files dated before 2026-11-24 keep their 45+ and 50+ titles and labels, because that is what they measured. 40–49 fit is recorded as `unknown`. Other games' READMEs are a mechanical follow-up.
