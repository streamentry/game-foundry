# I416: Fish rising

Run 3, batch 16.

In the warm months, a fish rises now and then: a brighter ring on the water, most often at the edges of the day (dawn, morning, golden hour, dusk: about every 5 seconds) and less in the middle of the day (about every 11). None at night or in winter. It's the reason the heron (I374) and kingfisher (I382) come, so the garden's logic is visible.
