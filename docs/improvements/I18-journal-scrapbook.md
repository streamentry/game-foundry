# I18 — Journal becomes a painted scrapbook

Date: 2026-11-24. Branch: feat/mosselora-painted-ui (PR #76).

**Player problem (I12 finding 5).** The journal ("Our choices") was a text list with small icons, like a log file. It is the player's own story, the thing most worth reopening.

**Change.** The journal is a **painted scrapbook**: pages two to a row on the Codex watercolor-paper tile, each with the painting of the choice (64 pt), a small portrait of the neighbor who remembers it pinned to the corner, and the written line beneath. An odd last page keeps half width. The icon strip at the top of the journal (I02) is dropped as redundant; the ending keeps it.

**Vision fit.** §3 "the place remembers it truthfully". The memories are pictures of people and places, not a list.

**Evidence.** b23 QA tour (`I18-scrapbook.png`): four pages after four choices, each with its painting, the rememberer's face and the line.
