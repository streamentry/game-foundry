# Next two experiments — Kakuro Compass and Quiet Camp

**Date:** 2026-11-22. **Instruction:** implement two additional distinct prototypes in Game Foundry. **Scope:** original offline-first Godot 4.7.2 projects with deterministic tests, WebGL2 artifacts and a separate design identity. Do not confuse working games with validated acquisition, retention or willingness to pay.

| Prototype | Existing research identifier | Player behavior tested | Strongest contrary case | Cheapest falsification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **6. Kakuro Compass** | **GF-001**, conditional first search-led choice | Does always-visible across/down *local* candidate feasibility improve first-board understanding versus established hint-driven Kakuro apps? | Major incumbents already advertise next-step hints, zoom, readable clues and beginner tutorials. A local clue inspector may simply reveal too much. | Five US adults 50–75 familiar with Kakuro; rotate first-game order against a mature incumbent, measure unaided correct placement and whether they can explain a crossing deduction. |
| **7. Quiet Camp** | **GF-006**, unvalidated candidate from search queue | Will visual row/column counting, orthogonal matching, and quiet tap-to-cycle tents produce a satisfying repeatable logic loop? | Tents & Trees is a known genre; a small original board set and unique solver do not demonstrate a reason to switch. | 6–10 adults 45+ on their own phones, compare rule comprehension and voluntary replay to an established Tents & Trees game. Check diagonal and pairing confusion. |

**Decision discipline:** the first five games already consumed engineering effort without external behavior measurements. These two are user-requested additional experiments and must not become a reason to postpone real playtests or to publish seven apps with unverified search demand. The first two next actions with maximum information value are one public link per game and a small head-to-head participant session.

**Implementation evidence:** small original board sets and deterministic solver tests establish correct Kakuro uniqueness and Tents & Trees matching/uniqueness. They do **not** establish difficulty distribution, age-specific usability, App Store keyword opportunity, net new users, retention or commercial economics. The separate browser ZIP artifact is not public hosting; native device testing remains open.

**Action threshold:** do not generate 100 more levels unless players demonstrate comprehensible first moves, preference versus an incumbent and unsponsored voluntary continuation or return. Explicitly record participant denominators, board version and any UX revision. Stop after one presentation revision without evidence of comparative advantage.
