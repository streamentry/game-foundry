# Global market priorities — word search, Shikaku, Kakuro

See the [first-game decision](first-game-decision-2026-11-21.md) for the newer U.K. and Japan measurements and the Kakuro recommendation.

The user selected three concepts for further research: accessible large-print word search, Shikaku, and Kakuro. Kakuro is the current **conditional first proof-of-play candidate**, not a production greenlight; Shikaku is the fallback. See the [Kakuro global ASO-only recheck](kakuro-global-aso-45plus-2026-11-21.md) for newer direct competitors and unmeasured local keyword synonyms. Adults 45+ remain the primary audience; first player research should include adults 50–75. Avoid treating age as a proxy for ability or preferences.

## Market selection

**Initial research markets:** United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia (English); Japan (Japanese); Germany (German); France (French); South Korea (Korean). This is a working research sequence, not a verified ranking of opportunities for these three games. Country-specific keyword demand, organic ranking, 45+ player fit and willingness to pay are unknown except for the earlier U.S. Google screen.

The English markets share a language but must be measured separately: search terms, competition, pricing and players differ. Japan is a priority for Shikaku and Kakuro, subject to validating local search terms and strong local incumbents. Germany and France add large European markets. South Korea merits a separate localized screen. Native review is required for terminology and word-game content; do not translate word lists mechanically and call them finished.

**China:** a major market, but defer a launch commitment until store access, local distribution requirements, content rules, localization and economics are verified. This is an operational prioritization, not a claim about demand. Other markets remain candidates after the initial screen.

Sensor Tower identified the U.S., China (iOS only in its estimate) and Japan as the largest mobile gaming consumer-spend markets in 2024 ([source](https://sensortower.com/press/press-release-sensor-tower-mobile-gaming-rebounds-in-2024-as-player-engagement-and-spending-reach-new-highs)). Its 2026 mobile overview describes the U.S. as the largest mobile market by revenue and highlights the UK, Germany and France in European growth ([report](https://download.s21i.co99.net/30295522/0/0/ABUIABA9GAAgrv3-zAYoiti5zgE.pdf?f=%5B%EC%98%81%EB%AC%B8%5D+Sensor+Tower+State+of+Mobile+2026.pdf&v=1772093102)). These cover broad mobile spending, not the addressable market for these games or for adults 45+.

## What is measured

In the U.S., Google Keyword Planner returned 1K–10K average monthly searches for each of `large print word search`, `shikaku puzzle`, and `kakuro puzzle` for November 2025–October 2026. These are Google web estimates with close variants; they do not measure app installs or age. The 30-query worksheet and method are in [the previous screen](45plus-market-screen-2026-11-21.md).

Existing apps offer large print, offline play, no ads, and hints. Examples include [large-print word search](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/large-print-word-search-daily/id6759038491), [Shikaku](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shikaku-rectangles/id6762503436), and [Kakuro](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kakuro-number-crossword/id1197071375). Listing features are developer claims and catalog presence does not establish search rank.

## Next research gate

For each game and country, record local exact terms and variants, source/date and keyword estimates, then capture the first ten unsponsored results in both stores where available. Sample recent positive and negative reviews, test the strongest concepts with adults 50–75, and validate original content rights. Prioritize one market–game pair only when demand, player need and a plausible route to discovery are all supported. Do not build three games or localize to every market by default.
