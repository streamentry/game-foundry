# Five game ideas to validate — 2026-11-21

These are research priorities, not approved games. Ranking is judgment based on the 30-query screen, public listings, feasible differentiation, and the 45+ strategy. App Store organic ranks, app-install intent, and 45+ interest remain unknown. Google ranges are web searches in Nov 2025–Oct 2026 and include close variants; they are not additive or downloads.

1. **Vietnamese Nối Từ with transparent word rulings** — Vietnam `nối từ`: 10K–100K/month. Test a calm, optional-timer word chain that explains disputed terms. Counterargument: [a direct competitor](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=vi&id=vn.comeo.noitu) reports 50K+ downloads and a larger dictionary responding to rejection complaints. Commercial lexicon rights and remaining error rate are unknown. Falsify with 20 balanced recent reviews and 200 independently adjudicated chains; park if the prior five-complaint / 98% agreement gates fail.
2. **Large-print word search with accessible selection** — U.S. `large print word search`: 1K–10K/month. Test adjustable grids, tap endpoints and readable content with 50–75-year-olds. [Existing products](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/large-print-word-search-daily/id6759038491) already offer these features; a credible advantage is unproven. Stop if target users cannot name and demonstrate a repeated unmet need against the leading apps.
3. **Shikaku with explainable, accessible placement** — U.S. `shikaku puzzle`: 1K–10K/month. Test tap-to-place and optional step explanations. [Existing apps](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shikaku-rectangles/id6762503436) already offer no ads and extensive content; preference for this wedge is unknown. Stop if participants complete comparable tasks as easily in incumbents or prefer them.
4. **Vietnamese Ô Ăn Quan for solo and local play** — Vietnam `ô ăn quan`: 1K–10K/month. Test familiar rules, readable board and optional local two-player play. Search may be for rules, schoolwork, or physical play; [an app already exists](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/o-an-quan-vietnamese-mancala/id6817187778). Stop if storefront audit and player interviews show no unresolved play need or insufficient app intent.
5. **Kakuro with teaching hints and adjustable zoom** — U.S. `kakuro puzzle`: 1K–10K/month. Test hints that explain the next deduction. [Conceptis](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kakuro-number-crossword/id1197071375) already offers zoom, sum combinations and no ads, with 1.9K ratings at 4.8. Stop if its hints already solve the observed need or players do not prefer the teaching approach.

Before selecting one for production, capture ten unsponsored results on each target storefront, sample around 20 recent positive and negative reviews across the relevant competitors, and interview or observe at least five players aged 50–75 per leading market. Five is an initial qualitative screen, not a representative estimate. Record actual participant ages and do not infer age from reviews. The Nối Từ lexicon also needs rights clearance. The physical audit is pending: iOS device discovery timed out and ADB failed to return a device list.

The previously selected first three remain the first physical audits; ideas four and five are backups. No candidate has been established as low competition or commercially viable.
