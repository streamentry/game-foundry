# Global game mechanics backlog: 50 speculative concepts (2027–2036)

> Status: **ideation only**. Added 2026-11-22. These are guesses about durable player motivations, **not** market forecasts, verified ASO demand, available trademarks, validated product names, approvals to build, or permission to ship.

## Relationship to the current game-foundry process

- Preserve the existing [100 keyword research hypotheses](candidates.csv) and their `GF-*` identifiers. The entries below use distinct `GC-*` identifiers; avoid double-counting mechanic overlap.
- Default studio research still prioritizes adults aged 45+, but these global concepts make **no verified age-fit claim**. Research an audience per concept before a build decision.
- A named concept can share mechanics with an existing prototype. Improve or compare against that prototype instead of opening a redundant product.
- Keep the first-game [Kakuro decision](../research/first-game-decision-2026-11-21.md) and [Good Eye global visual test](../research/global-45plus-top-three-games-2026-11-21.md) intact. New creative ideas do not override their evidence gates.
- Working title and brand availability need App Store, trademark and domain review before public use. No revenue, ranking, download, ASO, or retention claim is implied.

## Research-to-build gates

1. **Desk screen:** Identify primary player, competing games, observed complaints, and distribution mechanism (existing keyword, sharing, community, or paid acquisition). Record source and date; leave unavailable metrics **unknown**.
2. **Cheapest falsification:** Prototype one interaction and 5–10 original levels. Compare side-by-side with a relevant incumbent using target players; do not add AI or a backend unless essential to test the mechanic.
3. **Player evidence:** Observe first-minute comprehension, voluntary replay, frustration, and return on another day. Test adult 45+ participants where that audience is the claim; do not generalize from a younger sample.
4. **Commercial evidence:** Validate discoverability and intended monetization separately from enjoyment. Paid growth requires plausible unit economics; ASO-first candidates need observed relevant keyword demand.
5. **Decision:** `idea → desk-screened → prototype → playtested → commercial-test → approved/parked/rejected`. Do not automatically graduate a concept because it is easy to implement.

**Suggested starting allocation:** Time-box 3 mechanic tests (Unfold, One Move, Still) while preserving the incumbent comparison for Kakuro Compass/Zen. Stop adding parallel implementations if real player feedback is absent.

**Illustrative, not universal, exploratory thresholds:** Seek evidence of repeated voluntary play and player preference over a named alternative before expanding. A 30-person sample is directional only; report confidence intervals and context instead of declaring a winner from one percentage.

**Primary failure modes:** mechanic novelty decays, tiny ASO addressable demand, acquisition dominated by incumbents, store copycat rules, fragile content costs, accessibility regressions, and speculative brand collisions.
