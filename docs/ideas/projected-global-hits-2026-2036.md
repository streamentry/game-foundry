# Projected 50 global hits, 2026–2036 — original speculative IP portfolio

> **Source:** founder-supplied 50-title forecast, captured 2026-11-22. The ranking is *subjective prediction*, not a verified revenue/cultural-impact forecast, market research, existing release list, or evidence of title/IP availability. These are unvalidated working concepts, not approved builds.

## Portfolio intent and provenance

The supplied thesis emphasizes AI-native living worlds, remembered NPCs and persistent consequences, hybrid mobile gameplay with systems depth, player co-creation, and simulation. Preserve the founder's **rank ordering**; no artificial numerical scoring or evidence-backed probability is implied. New `PH-*` identifiers mark this **projected hits** portfolio separately from `GF-*` ASO hypotheses, `GC-*` original concepts, `GX-*` experimental mechanics, `BR-*` franchise comparisons and `FP-*` speculative reference titles.

**Core misconception to test:** “Feels alive” is a player judgment about coherent, consequential reactivity—not a requirement for unrestricted generative AI or infinite content.

## What to test first (not a build commitment)

The listed projects vary enormously in capital, distribution and live-ops requirements. Do **not** interpret their forecast rank as an indie build priority.

| Mechanism | Linked titles | Cheapest falsification experiment | Key blocker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Coherent NPC memory** | Echoes of Aether, Quiet Orbit, Legacy Kernel | Three NPCs with deterministic goals, editable history and causal events; blinded comparison to scripted NPCs | State consistency, privacy, inference cost |
| **Socially persistent shared places** | Solstice Collective, Orbit Collective, Driftwood | Two-player asynchronous settlement with 8 meaningful decisions and shared changelog | Multiplayer cold start, moderation, retention |
| **Hybrid mobile depth** | Evergrove, Pixel Sovereign, Bloom & Ruin | 60-second tactile core + one consequence-rich strategy decision; compare core-only retention | Meta bloat, acquisition economics |
| **Creator-first worlds** | Nexusforge, Lumen Arcade | One editable template-based world plus playable share link; measure recipient play and remix | Creator supply, distribution, UGC safety |
| **Bounded emergent strategy** | Chronicle Engine, Root & Circuit, Verdant Siege | Deterministic resource/agent simulation with comprehensible counterfactuals | Agent unpredictability, balancing and performance |

## Decision gates

1. **Deduplicate mechanics before implementation:** the overlaps in the table point to existing `GC`, `GX` and `LW` work; prefer extending proven code and validating a novel interaction over creating 50 new projects.
2. **Verify audience and distribution:** current repository emphasis is adults 45+, but these are broad global hypotheses. Record the user segment, competing alternatives, discovery channel, market and willingness to return.
3. **Start deterministic:** show reliable rule/state transitions first. Use LLMs only if controlled A/B player tests show incremental value above comparable crafted systems.
4. **Bound cost and trust:** assess NPC-memory storage, deletion, moderation, abusive interactions, performance, cross-play and creator marketplace risks before any large platform effort.
5. **Use real behavior:** measure first-minute understanding, replay, D1/D7 voluntary return, head-to-head preference and sharing; small sample observations are directional only.
6. **Reject or park:** if there is no demonstrated user preference, no realistic distribution wedge, or economics require platform-scale investment. A top-10 forecast entry is not exempt.

## Strongest opposing case

An exceptionally polished, authored puzzle, strategy title or RPG may outperform a generated living world. Players can value *predictability, fairness, artistic voice, coherent narrative and latency-free play* more than apparent infinity. AI abundance also expands competition and can make discovery harder. Global platform outcomes have winner-take-most dynamics and are not implied by their mechanics.
