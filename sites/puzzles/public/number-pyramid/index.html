Number Pyramid

Find the four digits, 1 to 9, along the bottom of a pyramid. Each of the six stones above equals the sum of the two directly beneath it, and a few clue labels are fixed to guide you.

Select a bottom stone and enter a digit; the stones above show their sums as you go.

Six original boards, each unlocking the next once solved. Undo and Restart are always available, there is no timer, and your progress saves on this device. Plays offline. No ads, no account, no data collection.
