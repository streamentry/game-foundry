Cage Hearth

Fill a 4×4 grid with 1 to 4 so that no digit repeats in any row or column. Each coloured cage shows a target built from a sum, product, difference, quotient or single value, and the digits inside must reach it.

Tap a cell, then enter a digit on the large keypad. Erase clears a cell.

Six original boards, each unlocking the next once solved. Undo and Restart are always available, there is no timer, and your progress saves on this device. Plays offline. No ads, no account, no data collection.

Your progress
