Futoshiki Terrace

Fill a 4×4 grid with the numbers 1 to 4 so each appears once in every row and column, while every greater-than or less-than sign between neighbouring cells holds true.

Tap an editable cell, then one of four large digit buttons. Erase and Next are always visible.

Six original boards, each unlocking the next once solved. Undo and Restart are always available, there is no timer, and your progress saves on this device. Plays offline. No ads, no account, no data collection.
