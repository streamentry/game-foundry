Kakuro Compass

Fill each Kakuro run with digits 1–9 so its total matches the clue; do not repeat a digit within a run. Solve eight original 5×5 boards to unlock the next. The Compass inspector shows across and down totals and locally feasible digits for the selected cell. Undo, erase, restart, and local progress are included.
