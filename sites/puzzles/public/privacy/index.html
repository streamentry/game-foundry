Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026. This policy covers every puzzle game listed on this site, published by CLOUDJET SOLUTIONS PTE. LTD. on the App Store and Google Play.

What we collect

Nothing. The games play fully offline. They contain no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, no account or sign-in, and they do not request internet access. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Data kept on your device

Each game saves your puzzle progress and settings in its own private storage on your device so you can continue where you left off. That file never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The games are made for adults and are suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected, no data from children is collected.
