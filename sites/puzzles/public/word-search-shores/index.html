Word Search Shores

Find three target words hidden in a 6×6 grid of letters. Each word runs in a straight line, horizontally, vertically or diagonally, and appears exactly once.

Tap the first and last letters of a word, in either direction. Found words stay highlighted.

Six original boards, each unlocking the next once solved. Undo and Restart are always available, there is no timer, and your progress saves on this device. Plays offline. No ads, no account, no data collection.
