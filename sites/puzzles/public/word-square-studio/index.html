Word Square Studio

Arrange three 3-letter English words into a 3×3 square so that each column reads the same word as the matching row.

Tap the unplaced word cards in order from top to bottom; the board shows each row as you place it.

Six original boards, each unlocking the next once solved. Undo and Restart are always available, there is no timer, and your progress saves on this device. Plays offline. No ads, no account, no data collection.
