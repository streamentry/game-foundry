Akari Evening

Place lamps on a 4×4 board so that every open cell is lit, no two lamps shine on each other, and each numbered wall touches exactly that many lamps.

Tap an open cell to cycle through lamp, cross note and empty; lit cells are highlighted as you go.

Six original boards, each unlocking the next once solved. Undo and Restart are always available, there is no timer, and your progress saves on this device. Plays offline. No ads, no account, no data collection.
